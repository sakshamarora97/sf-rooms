How Unlisted Rooms works

Where do listings come from?
Unlisted Rooms organizes housing posts from relevant Facebook groups.
Does Unlisted Rooms host these listings?
No. Every result links back to the original Facebook post — that's where you contact the poster.
Are listings verified?
No. Unlisted Rooms organizes third-party posts but does not independently verify landlords, posters, properties, or availability.
How often are posts updated?
New posts are checked roughly hourly. That means new posts appear quickly — it doesn't mean every existing listing's availability has been reconfirmed.
Safety
Verify the poster and the property yourself before sending any money.
