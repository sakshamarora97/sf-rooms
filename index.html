How it works

  • We read the groups. Every hour we collect new posts from public Facebook housing groups in each city.
  • We organize them. An AI model pulls out the rent, neighborhood, room type, dates and amenities, and sorts posts into listings, room seekers and spam.
  • You filter and click through. Search by neighborhood, rent and dates, see results on a map, and open the original Facebook post to contact the poster.

Frequently asked questions

What is Unlisted Rooms?

A way to search the room rentals, subleases, lease takeovers and roommate searches people post in Facebook groups. We don't host listings; every result links back to the original post.

Where do the listings come from?

Public Facebook housing groups for San Francisco, the East Bay including Berkeley, and the New York City area including Jersey City and Hoboken.

How often is it updated?

Hourly between 6 AM and 10 PM Pacific time. Each page shows when it was last updated.

Do I need an account?

No. You can browse and filter freely. An optional account lets you save listings and keep private notes and tags.

Are the posts verified?

No. Posts come straight from Facebook groups. Always confirm details with the poster, and be careful with deposits and payments.
